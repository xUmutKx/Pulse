## Pulse 1.0.80

Changes since 1.0.30: the keyboard no longer drops a typed word or throws the cursor elsewhere when an app moves it; Messages: the new-message button sits at the bottom right; the keyboard is lower when the phone is turned sideways; Motion cues use the sensor fusion of Motion cues by David Ventura (GPL-3.0): a dot field fixed in the world, the middle of the screen can stay clear, taps pass through on Android 12; the keyboard translation bar has its own input (the keys write there, the translation goes into the text box); Palette, a new extra app (icon pack studio: a palette from a mood, the wallpaper, Material You or at random, a style, how colours go to apps, a preview, icons saved as pictures); the Pulse launcher gets icon packs inside Pulse (Palette look, Material glass, theme colour only, and Windows 11 / 10 / 7 / XP / 95 icons); Weather: sky and AMOLED looks that follow the weather, a list of places with a swipe between them, pollen, UV, humidity, wind compass, pressure, dew point, visibility, today against yesterday, 48 hours and a refresh in the background every 30 minutes; Files: a Recordings/Call page (the people called, calls by day, play in place), music files play in a small player with speed, share and archive menus, extension button, HTML pages shown in the app; Files: share several files at once; Files: every row of an open archive has a three-dot menu (open, cut, copy, paste, delete) and the selection bar can paste and delete; Poweramp: load the album_art and artist_art folders (covers by "Artist - Title.jpg", artist pictures by "Artist.jpg" shown in the artists list, also from a backup zip); keyboard: swapped letters ("wrok") are always mended, clipboard long press (pin, shortcut, select) and shortcut names offered as suggestions, panels keep the height of the keys, an empty text box starts with a capital, the emoji pager settles, categories on top and compact presets, Material install window; Files home opens with the last numbers at once; Files: "Open archive" opens the full-page archive view, an "Open as" dialog in the Material style, swipe actions on the File types list (delete, open or select, per direction) instead of a trash button; Material dialogs use plain text buttons, Material buttons are tonal, and the remaining direct dialogs go through the same theme code; Toolkit: Floating keys (a button over every app, a mini keyboard with Esc, Tab, Ctrl and F keys; needs root or Shizuku); keyboard: translation bar, effects (colour wash, key colour, key motion, touch sounds), many more accents on long press, optional compact bottom row with Shift, separate top and bottom brightness, a clearer Shift key, symbol families above the search, recent emojis stay in place while the panel is open, pop-ups no longer cut off on the number row, deleting the space behind a word reopens it, more careful typo learning, 131 typos built in.

**Which file:** `Pulse-1.0.80.apk` is for Android 12 and newer. `Pulse-1.0.80-android5.apk` is the build for older phones (Android 5 and newer, "low API"). Both hold the arm64-v8a, armeabi-v7a and x86_64 libraries. Builds are signed with the fixed key in `ci/pulse.keystore`, so every release installs over the last one. Both builds were compiled in CI; neither has been tested on every phone, so please report bugs.
